QuietScan — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

QuietScan ("the app"), published by Sycamore Hill Studios, is a local-only app.

  • No data collection. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no analytics code and no advertising SDK.
  • Scanning happens on your device. QR codes and barcodes are decoded entirely on your phone by a reader bundled inside the app. Camera frames are processed in memory and never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.
  • Camera. The camera is used only while you are scanning, only to find codes. You can decline the permission and still generate codes, browse history, or type a code by hand.
  • Scan history. Your scan history is stored only on your device. You can delete any entry or all of it, and Incognito mode saves nothing at all.
  • Opening links. The app never opens a scanned link by itself. It shows you the address first; if you choose to open it, it opens in your own browser, outside the app.
  • No accounts. The app requires no sign-up.
  • Purchases. Optional tips are processed entirely by Google Play's billing system. The app never sees or stores your payment details. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Deleting data. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

On iPhone

  • Same promise. QuietScan for iPhone collects nothing either: no account, no analytics, no ads and no network features. Your scan history and settings stay in the app's private storage on your iPhone.
  • Camera. Codes are decoded on your iPhone by Apple's built-in reader. Camera frames are processed in memory and never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
  • Photos. Scanning from a photo uses the iOS photo picker, so QuietScan can't see the rest of your library. Saving a generated code to Photos uses add-only access.
  • Links. A scanned link opens in your browser, outside the app, only after you tap Open.
  • Device backups. Like any app's data, it is included in your own iCloud Backup or computer backup if you have those turned on. Those backups belong to you and follow Apple's terms; we never see them.
  • Purchases. Optional supporter packs are one-time purchases processed entirely by Apple's App Store. The app never sees or stores your payment details; the App Store only tells it which pack your Apple Account owns, so it can unlock that pack's colors and art on your iPhone. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Ratings. If you're ever asked to rate the app, that request is shown by iOS itself.
  • Deleting data. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes everything it stored there.
